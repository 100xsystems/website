# Fundamentals of Node.js

## What Is Node.js?

Node.js is an important concept in the Runtimes domain of software engineering. Understanding it deeply helps you design better systems, communicate more effectively with your team, and make informed architectural decisions.

### Why It Matters

- **Shared vocabulary** — Teams that understand Node.js can communicate complex ideas succinctly
- **Better designs** — Knowing when and how to apply Node.js leads to more maintainable systems
- **Industry standard** — Node.js is widely referenced in system design interviews, architecture reviews, and technical discussions

## Core Concepts

### The Basic Idea

At its heart, Node.js is about addressing a recurring challenge in software design. By understanding the core mechanism, you can recognize opportunities to apply it in your own work.

### Key Characteristics

1. **Problem-focused** — Node.js exists to solve a specific class of problems
2. **Proven approach** — It has been validated through years of practical use
3. **Context-dependent** — The effectiveness depends on your specific situation

## When to Use It

Node.js is most valuable when:

* You encounter the specific problem it addresses
* The trade-offs align with your system's requirements
* Your team has the expertise to implement it correctly

## Common Misconceptions

- **"Node.js solves everything"** — No single approach works in every context
- **"Node.js is outdated"** — Many classic concepts remain highly relevant
- **"Node.js is too complex"** — The fundamentals are straightforward; depth comes with practice

## Practice Questions

1. Can you describe Node.js in your own words to a junior developer?
2. What problem does Node.js solve that would exist without it?
3. What are the trade-offs of using Node.js?
4. Can you think of a real system that exemplifies Node.js?
