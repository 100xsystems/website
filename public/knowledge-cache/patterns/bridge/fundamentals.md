### Why It Matters

- **Shared vocabulary** — Teams that understand Bridge can communicate complex ideas succinctly
- **Better designs** — Knowing when and how to apply Bridge leads to more maintainable systems
- **Industry standard** — Bridge is widely referenced in system design interviews, architecture reviews, and technical discussions

## Core Concepts

### The Basic Idea

At its heart, Bridge is about addressing a recurring challenge in software design. By understanding the core mechanism, you can recognize opportunities to apply it in your own work.

### Key Characteristics

1. **Problem-focused** — Bridge exists to solve a specific class of problems
2. **Proven approach** — It has been validated through years of practical use

## When to Use It

Bridge is most valuable when:

* You encounter the specific problem it addresses
* The trade-offs align with your system's requirements
